HOW TO READ THIS

The bar shows completed assigned courses as a share of everything assigned to that unit, not a share of colleagues. Units differ enormously in how much is assigned to them, so a smaller unit moves its bar with far fewer completions than a large one. Read it as momentum, not as a scoreboard.

HOW TO READ THIS

The bar counts people, not courses: the share of current colleagues in each unit with at least one completed course since 1 January 2026. The right-hand figure is the change in percentage points since August. Small units move faster, because one colleague can shift them by several points. Read it as momentum, not as a scoreboard.

Iraq, Algeria and Nigeria are not on the board because they do not have access to Cornerstone.

# Explainer script: Same page (production revision 2)

Refresher film for Topic 4, Their view first. Revised Nov 19, 2026 after the jury review of the first cut. The treatment, cut list, LMS question and controlling idea are unchanged from the source script (`script_source.md`). This version records what the film now does and where it departs from the source.

## What changed from the source script, and why

| Change | Source | Now | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Length | 80 s | 80 s, including a 2 s end card | The first cut ran 83 s. The opening hold and the slack after the say-back are gone; the first exchange keeps its extra reading time. |
| His view bubble | 30 words, including "I'll move the start to half ten" | "Then position two is on hold until I revise it, because your crew plans from this sequence." | The source's own fallback. The move to 10:30 is shown as the start time sliding on the sheet. |
| The trucks on the plan | Two flat trucks | One side-view flatbed icon sitting on P2, the same shape as the truck on the quay | One truck reads at a glance; the dialogue still says "trucks". |
| Accent colour | Mira's bubble and the 6-to-10 arc | Mira's bubbles are yellow; the arc is yellow inside a blue-deep clock disc; a yellow ring flashes around the sheet for four frames as the copies lock | Yellow on the white sheet fails contrast; inside a dark disc and outside the sheet it passes. |
| Step labels | Plain on-screen text | Three blue pills under the sheet that build the rule | Keeps the rule visually apart from the story text without a second typeface (the design system allows one per film). |
| End card | Sarens Academy end card | Silent card: "Topic 4 refresher / Their view first", logo slot empty | No logo file exists, and the design system forbids typing the name in its place. Drop in MarCom artwork when it exists. |
| Narration timing | VO over the say-back | VO starts 12 frames after the paper click | The lock lands on its own sound, as the animator notes ask. |

## Animator notes: crane safety (new)

Operations people will watch this. Every crane frame must pass an operations SME.

- The crane travels with the boom stowed and the hook secured short. The boom goes up only after the crane has stopped on its position.
- People stay at least 300 px (a crane length) clear of any moving crane or truck, behind a barrier line. No vehicle ever passes between people and the camera or through them.
- The truck leaves before the crane moves in. Never two vehicles moving at once on the quay.
- Outriggers set before any lift; the film ends before a load is picked.

## Production notes

- Voice: recorded VO (ElevenLabs, Marie), placed sentence by sentence on the picture; every line also runs as a caption. The file is band-limited at about 11 kHz; re-export at 48 kHz or record a human read before launch.
- Typeface: Caslon only (design system rule 4).
- Music: placeholder bed synthesised in the build, ducked about 12 dB under the voice, with hard cut-outs on the paper click and the four outrigger clunks. Replace with a licensed track before launch.
- Open items: SME sign-off on the crane frames and on the LMS question (Joris, convoy lead, permit time windows), the series log, the logo.

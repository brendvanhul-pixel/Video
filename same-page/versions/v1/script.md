# Explainer script: Same page (production revision)

Refresher film for Topic 4, Their view first. Revised Nov 19, 2026 against the build. The treatment, cut list, LMS question and controlling idea are unchanged from the source script (`script_source.md`). This version records what the film now does, frame for frame, and why it departs from the source where it does.

## What changed from the source script, and why

| Change | Source | Now | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Length | 80 s | 83 s | The first exchange (Mira 17 words, him 8) had 7 s while the bubbles moved. A viewer who cannot read Mira's point cannot see the film's argument. The three seconds go there. |
| His view bubble at 0:57 | 30 words, including "I'll move the start to half ten" | "Then position two is on hold until I revise it, because your crew plans from this sequence." | The source's own fallback. The move to 10:30 is shown as the start time sliding on the sheet, so nothing is lost. |
| Accent colour | Mira's bubble and the 6-to-10 arc | Mira's bubbles are yellow; the arc is yellow inside a blue-deep clock disc | Yellow on the white sheet fails contrast. Inside a dark disc it passes, and it is still her colour landing on the shared page. |
| End card | Sarens Academy end card | Ends on the callback sheet and the rule | The design system has no logo file and forbids typing the name in its place. Add MarCom artwork when it exists. |
| Ask and view rows | 0:48 and 0:54 | 0:53.6 and 0:58 | The recorded VO line "That isn't agreeing. It puts you both on one page." runs to 0:53.5. The script's rule is that narration never runs while a bubble is being read, so the bubbles wait. |
| Freeze question | Not placed | Under the copies, where the rule row later builds | At the top it collided with Mira's bubble, which the viewer must still be able to read at that moment. |

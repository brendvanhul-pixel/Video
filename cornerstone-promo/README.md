# Learn anywhere: Cornerstone app promo (9:16, 27 s)

`index.html` is the whole video as one time-driven scene (`render(t)`). Open it in a browser to preview the loop;
`index.html?still=26` freezes on a moment. `node render.mjs` renders 30 fps frames with Playwright and encodes
`out/cornerstone-learn-anywhere-9x16.mp4` plus `out/poster-end-card.png`. `node render.mjs --still 9.5` exports one frame.

Brand assets not in this public repo (add to `assets/` before rendering): `crane-vertical.jpg`, `operator-dusk.jpg`
(both from the Sarens brand template) and `sarens-logo-white.png`.

QR codes in `assets/` point to the Cornerstone app on Google Play (`com.csod.learning`) and the Belgian App Store (`id970204620`).

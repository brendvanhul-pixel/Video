# Proof clip: "What is a performance review?"

24.0 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC 48 kHz stereo. Text-only (no VO). Run `./build.sh` to rebuild `video.mp4` from source in about 1 minute.

## Concept
A yellow circle is the only thing that carries the film from start to finish. It is the dot in "?", it rolls through the year, it opens the iris into the meeting room, and it lands as the full stop in the end title. The message is that a review is a planned, two-way conversation with three parts.
